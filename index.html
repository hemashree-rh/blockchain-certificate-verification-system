How The System Works

Zero paper forgery. Zero manual phone calls. Complete cryptographic truth.

1

College Issues Certificate

Authorized college administrator inputs student details and uploads the digital certificate. A unique 256-bit cryptographic hash is computed.

2

Smart Contract Records Hash

The certificate hash, ID, and verification metadata are stored permanently on the Ethereum blockchain. Data is immutable and cannot be tampered with.

3

Instant Public Verification

Anyone (employers, colleges, embassies) uploads the certificate or types the ID. The system matches the cryptographic hash directly against the blockchain.

About This Project

A B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering Final/Mini Project on Decentralized Identity & Verification.

The Problem of Fake Certificates

Over 20% of resumes and paper degrees submitted in competitive job markets contain forged marks, fake degrees, or doctored timestamps. Traditional verification requires tedious phone calls, physical postal mail, or expensive third-party background verification agencies that take weeks.

Why Blockchain is the Solution

Blockchain provides an immutable, decentralized, and append-only ledger. Once an authorized college administrator records a certificate hash on-chain, it cannot be edited, deleted, or forged by any unauthorized actor—not even server administrators.

Student Privacy by Design

Sensitive student personal identification documents are never stored directly on the public blockchain. Instead, only a 32-byte cryptographic SHA-256 digest is stored. The original document remains private with the student while remaining 100% mathematically verifiable.

Technology Stack

  • Frontend: HTML5, Responsive CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
  • Web3 Library: Ethers.js v5.7
  • Smart Contract: Solidity 0.8.20
  • Development Environment: Remix IDE & Node.js Express
  • Wallet: MetaMask Web3 Browser Extension
  • Hashing Algorithm: Client-Side SHA-256 (Web Crypto API)
